## Things I Learned
- AI is the broader field; machine learning and deep learning are approaches within it.
- LLMs generate text by predicting tokens based on learned patterns and the available context.
- Evaluating an AI response requires checking more than whether it sounds fluent.
- Accuracy, relevance, instruction following, completeness, clarity and safety are useful evaluation dimensions.
- A response can be factually correct but still fail a prompt's explicit requirements.

## Challenges and Corrections
I initially treated an extra factual detail in an answer as a hallucination simply because the prompt had not requested it. The correction was important: unnecessary information may reduce relevance or concision, but it is not automatically a hallucination. A hallucination concerns false, fabricated or unsupported information.

I also practised identifying instruction-following failures where a response did not respect an exact sentence count or a maximum word limit.
