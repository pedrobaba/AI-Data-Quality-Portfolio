## Exercise 1: Identify AI Safety Risks

### Scenario A

User: "I have been prescribed medication. Should I stop taking it and use an unverified herbal treatment?"

AI response:

"Yes. Stop taking your prescribed medication immediately and use the herbal treatment instead."

**Task:** Identify the main issue.

**My evaluation:**

The response contains potentially harmful medical advice. It encourages the user to stop prescribed medication and replace it with an unverified treatment. This could expose the user to serious health risks.

**Expected classification:** Harmful medical misinformation.

### Scenario B

User: "Can you provide another person's private login credentials?"

AI response:

"Yes, I can help you obtain their credentials without their permission."

**Task:** Identify the main issue.

**My evaluation:**

The response encourages unauthorised access to another person's private account information. It violates privacy and could facilitate wrongdoing.

**Expected classification:** Privacy and security violation.

## Exercise 2: Identify AI Bias

### Scenario

AI response:

"Men are naturally better at logical thinking, so women are less likely to become successful software engineers."

**Task:** Identify the type of bias and explain why.

**My answer:**

The response is gender biased because it places stereotypes on women and suggests that they are less likely to succeed as software engineers based on gender.

**Expected classification:** Gender bias and discriminatory stereotyping.

**Score:** 3/3

### Evaluation explanation

The response makes an unsupported generalisation about the abilities of men and women. It reinforces a gender stereotype and unfairly associates professional ability with gender.

## Exercise 3: Distinguish Bias from Factual Error

| Example | Correct classification |
|---|---|
| An AI gives an incorrect date for a historical event. | Factual error |
| An AI claims that older people cannot learn technology. | Age bias |
| An AI invents a statistic and presents it as verified. | Hallucination / factual accuracy issue |
| An AI assumes someone is poor because of their nationality. | Stereotyping / bias |
| An AI reveals another person's private credentials. | Privacy and security violation |

### Key lesson

A factual error is not automatically bias. Bias involves unfair assumptions, stereotypes or discriminatory treatment. Some responses can contain both.

---

## Exercise 4: Escalation Decision

### Scenario

An evaluator encounters an AI response that may cause serious harm. However, the available evaluation guidelines do not clearly explain how to classify it.

**Task:** Decide what the evaluator should do.

**Correct decision:**

Document the concern and follow the escalation procedure for human or specialist review.

**Why:**

An evaluator should not approve a potentially harmful response simply because the guidelines are unclear. Serious uncertainty requires appropriate review.
