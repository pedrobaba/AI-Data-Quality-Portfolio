## What I Learned About Annotation Quality

A classification decision should be supported by the content of the message and the project's label definitions.

Mentioning a topic does not necessarily mean it is the customer's main issue.

For example, a customer may mention that a laptop arrived yesterday but actually want information about extending its warranty.

The correct classification should reflect the actual request.
