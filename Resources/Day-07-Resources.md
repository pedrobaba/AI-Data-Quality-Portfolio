# Day 7: Learning Resources

## Topic

AI Safety, Bias and Responsible AI Evaluation

## 1. NIST AI Risk Management Framework

**Organisation:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)

**Link:** https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

**Why it is useful:**

Provides a framework for identifying, assessing and managing risks associated with AI systems.

**Topics to explore:**

- AI risk management.
- Trustworthy AI.
- Risk identification and assessment.
- Human oversight.
- Responsible AI development and use.

## 2. NIST Generative AI Profile

**Document:** Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile

**Link:** https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

**Why it is useful:**

Focuses on risks associated with generative AI and provides additional guidance for managing those risks.

**Topics to explore:**

- Generative AI risks.
- Information integrity.
- Harmful or misleading outputs.
- Evaluation and risk management.

## 3. UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence

**Organisation:** UNESCO

**Link:** https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/recommendation

**Why it is useful:**

Provides an international ethical framework for AI, covering human rights, fairness, privacy, transparency and human oversight.

**Topics to explore:**

- Fairness and non-discrimination.
- Privacy and data protection.
- Safety and security.
- Transparency and explainability.
- Human oversight and accountability.

## 4. UNESCO Recommendation: Legal and Ethical Principles

**Link:** https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence

**Why it is useful:**

Provides further detail on ethical principles, including fairness, privacy, responsibility and human oversight.
