# Day 6 Learning Journal – Human Feedback, Preference Ranking and RLHF

## What I Learned

Today, I learned how human feedback contributes to improving AI systems.

I understood that preference ranking involves comparing AI-generated responses and identifying which response better satisfies the user's requirements.

I also learned the meaning of RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) and how human preferences can be used as a learning signal during AI model optimisation.

## What I Practised

I completed practical exercises involving:

- Comparing two AI-generated responses.
- Ranking three responses.
- Identifying factual inaccuracies.
- Checking instruction following.
- Recognising unsafe recommendations.
- Writing evaluator justifications.

## My Assessment Performance

Total score: 10/12 (83.3%).

My strongest performance was the guided preference-ranking exercise, where I scored 3/3.

I also demonstrated an understanding of how to identify the best and worst responses.

## Challenges I Experienced

I initially struggled with:

1. Distinguishing between a response that is technically correct and one that follows the user's instructions.
2. Ranking responses consistently.
3. Identifying when a response contains an unsupported claim.
4. Providing sufficiently specific explanations for my decisions.

## What I Need to Improve

I need to become more consistent when evaluating responses against multiple criteria.

I should also identify specific evidence instead of giving general explanations such as "the response is not accurate."

## My Key Takeaway

A good AI evaluator should not select a response simply because it sounds professional or contains more information.

The decision must be based on the user's instructions, factual accuracy, relevance, clarity, completeness and safety.
