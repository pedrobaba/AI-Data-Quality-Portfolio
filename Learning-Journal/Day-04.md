## Things I Learned

Today I learned that evaluating an AI response requires more than checking whether the answer sounds correct.

A response can be factually accurate but still fail to follow the user's instructions.

I also learned that relevance and completeness are different. A response may discuss the correct topic but omit information specifically requested by the user.

Another important lesson was that complex language does not automatically make a response better. The response must be appropriate for its intended audience.

## Challenges

My main challenge was Exercise 2.

I initially marked the API testing response as irrelevant and inaccurate. After reviewing the evaluation criteria, I understood that the response was actually relevant and generally accurate but incomplete because it did not mention the two requested tools.

## Key Takeaways

1. Accuracy is different from instruction following.
2. Relevance is different from completeness.
3. Clear language improves usability.
4. A response should be judged against the original prompt.
5. Evaluation should be based on evidence rather than personal preference.
