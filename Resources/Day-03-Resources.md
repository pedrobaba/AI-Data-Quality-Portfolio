## 4. Practical Verification Checklist

Before accepting an AI-generated factual claim, ask:

- Can I identify the exact claim?
- Is there reliable evidence?
- Is the source credible?
- Are the names and dates correct?
- Are statistics supported by a verifiable source?
- Does the response contain both correct and incorrect claims?
- Is the information supported, contradicted or unverified?
